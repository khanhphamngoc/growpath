Users & Product Design
User Model
Parent Leads journey at ages 8–13. Sees the full delta view. Shifts to coach as child grows. Full child ownership at 16–18.
Child Participant → Co-owner → Full owner. Picks weekly/monthly/quarterly skill focus. Sees only empowerment, never conflict.
School Referral broker only. Zero data access. Earns incentive per active family enrolled.
Age 8–13 MVP Full control at 16–18 Vietnamese families
Conflict Resolution Model
1
Parent inputs their observations of the child
2
Child inputs their own self-perception
3
Delta surfaced to parent only — child never sees conflict
4
AI facilitates dialogue between parent and child
5
If unresolved: child's view wins for trial period → inspect & adapt later

Skill Progress Measurement
Evidence portfolio — child uploads real-world artifacts
Dual assessment — child + parent both rate skill confidence
Situational scenarios — AI generates real-world situations to reveal thinking
AI synthesis — infers skill confidence curve over time
AI + parent guidance — targeted learning topics from scenario performance
Engagement Loop
Weekly
Child check-in: activity log + reflection (5–10 min) + Parent digest with progress + conversation starter
Mid-week
AI nudges: skill prompts + situational scenarios dropped as surprise engagement, not chore
Monthly
Progress review against milestones — see how far they've come
Quarterly
Inspect & adapt — persona realignment + journey adjustment + new skill focus
Always
Milestone celebrations and effort recognition — celebration tied to trying and showing up, not just achieving
